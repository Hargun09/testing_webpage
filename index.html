Our Science

Research Focus Areas

01
🔬
Systemic Vasculitis

Investigating the immunopathogenesis of Kawasaki Disease and Deficiency of Adenosine Deaminase 2 (DADA2). We use Mult-omics techniques to characterize immune infiltrates in vessel walls and identify novel therapeutic targets.

ANCA-AAV Giant Cell Arteritis scRNA-seq Neutrophil Biology
02
🧫
Mucosal Immunology

Studying barrier immunity at gut and airway mucosa, focusing on ILC subsets, tissue-resident memory T cells, and the microbiome–immune axis in inflammatory disease. We integrate multi-omics with clinical biopsy cohorts.

ILC Biology Trm Cells Gut-Vessel Axis Microbiome
03
💊
Translational Biomarkers

Developing blood and tissue biomarkers for disease activity and treatment response in systemic vasculitis. Leveraging mass cytometry, Olink proteomics, and machine learning for clinical decision support tools.

CyTOF Olink Biomarker Discovery ML Models
04
🧬
Bioinformatics & Multi-omics

Building computational pipelines for integrative analysis of transcriptomic, proteomic, and epigenomic datasets. Open-source tools for the wider immunology community, with a focus on reproducible research.

Seurat / Scanpy CITE-seq Epigenomics R / Python
